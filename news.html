2020

  • June, 15th, 2020 : Our team at Samsung AI Cambridge (Juan-Manuel Perez-Rua, Brais Martinez, Victor Escorcia, Li Zhang, Xiatian Zhu, Tao Xiang and myself) has received an award for the third place in the Epic Kitchens challenge at CVPR 2020! The challenge consists in recognizing actions on egocentric video data taken in kitchens. The actions are compositional (i.e made of a verb and a noun) with a total of 44,000 possible actions. The paper that explains our method is available here.
